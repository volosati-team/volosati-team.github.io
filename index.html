Lisa Agent

Lisa Agent is a personal assistant application. It runs on infrastructure owned and operated by the account holder and is used by a single person — the account holder — to work with their own Google data.

It connects to Google services only with the account holder's explicit consent, on the account holder's own Google account, to read and edit the account holder's own email and spreadsheets when the account holder asks it to.

The application is not offered to the public, has no other users, and does not sell, share or transfer any Google user data to third parties.
